# Limitations

ResSim is educational and research software, not a qualified tool for reserves booking,
operational, investment, regulatory, or safety-critical decisions. Its models and references cover
specific simplified cases; agreement in a plotted result does not establish accuracy for other
reservoirs, fluids, wells, grids, controls, or timesteps.

Use independently verified software and professional review for real assets or decisions. Validation
evidence is indexed in the [documentation index](DOCUMENTATION_INDEX.md), with known gaps tracked in
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sergeyfarin/ressim/issues).
