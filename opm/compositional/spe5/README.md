# SPE5 Case 1 payloads (#52)

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `case.json` | The exact `CompositionalCaseConfig` the withheld `comp_spe5_wag` scenario sends. Exported from the TS definition and checked against it by the scenario test. |
| `stall_192.json` | The accepted checkpoint at month 192 (start of the water year in which the #93 Newton stall occurred). Seed for the month-192 → 201 regression; not a validated reference. Regenerate with `SPE5_CHECKPOINT_OUT=opm/compositional/spe5/stall_192.json` on the 16-year replay test. |
| `stall_call_058.json` | The accepted state at 6114.808 d, immediately before the failing #93 call. Fast regression state for the near-critical stability fix; captured from the pre-fix replay and deliberately not regenerated. Not a validated reference. |
| `stall_218.json` | The accepted state at 6612.820363 d, where the from-scratch replay's Newton iteration starts flipping cell 33 between single-liquid and two-phase (#116). Frozen seed for that investigation; not a validated reference. |
| `stall_218_from_scratch.json` | The accepted state at 6612.703446 d, immediately before the from-scratch replay's failing month-218 call (#116 failure B: ten halvings from 22.67 d, the last attempt a residual floor with no phase flips). Written by `comp_spe5_from_scratch_month218_failure_trace` (`SPE5_TRACE_OUT`) on `2ef5027a` plus behaviour-neutral step-0 instruments; `comp_spe5_from_scratch_218_seed_reproduces_signature_b` replays the call in about a minute. Frozen seed; not a validated reference. |
| `stall_215_memory.json` | The accepted state at 6540.217531 d from the timestep-memory experiment, where cell 32's flash exhausts its iteration cap (#119). Frozen seed for that flash regression; not a validated reference. |
| `reference.json` | The independent JutulDarcy 0.3.7 compositional run of the same `case.json` payload: 240 monthly reports (7305 d) at 30.4375 d, explicit 101325 Pa / 288.71 K surface conditions, producer rates rebuilt from component mass rates, per-report Jutul Newton/timestep diagnostics, and the first declared stop (month 207, water cut). Wells use JutulDarcy's default node volume (0.479 m³ producer, 0.192 m³ per injector) since 2026-11-08, **provisionally**: the volume is a model choice (ResSim's wells have no wellbore storage), and the earlier 1e-4 m³ nodes stalled the gas years on well-node CNV. The volume/timestep sensitivity (`tools/jutul/run-spe5-sensitivity.sh`, recorded in `docs/SPE5_VALIDATION.md`, #116) does not justify promoting it: at a 0.5 d cap the two volumes agree through month 217, but this report-step run is not converged in time (24% in producer oil from the 0.5 d run). Generated by `tools/jutul/spe5_reference.jl`; regenerate/verify with `bash tools/opm_compositional/run-spe5.sh [--check]`. |
| `cross_flash_205_216.json` | #116 cross-flash evidence: for cells 32/33/39/40 at monthly reports 205–216, ResSim's and JutulDarcy's own `(p, z)` and the swapped pairs (192 points), each flashed by ResSim and by MultiComponentFlash (tight `SSINewtonFlash` 1e-12 and Jutul's in-simulator default, with convergence flags, stability reports and every logged warning; forced splits with their Gibbs difference where Jutul calls a point single-phase). Summary counts originals and swaps separately. The merge refuses to write unless all 192 points have valid results from both flashes (no error or warning, a consistent stability report); `python3 -I tools/jutul/spe5_cross_flash_fixture.py check` re-validates the written fixture. Regenerate or verify with `bash tools/jutul/run-spe5-cross-flash.sh [--check]`; `comp_spe5_cross_flash_fixture_reproduces_the_ressim_column` fails if the ResSim flash moves its column. Provenance records the commit and dirty-tree flag, and `--check` on a clean tree adds a `verification` block with the commit, JutulDarcy configuration and source hashes. Evidence, not a validated reference. |
| `manifest.json` | Generator and verify commands, the commit the fixture was generated from, the dirty-tree flag, the resolved JutulDarcy configuration (`configuration`: per-well node volumes, timestep cap, versions), SHA-256 hashes of the reference, case, generator and Julia environment, and, after a `--check` on a clean tree, the verifying commit and hashes (`verification`). The wrapper refuses to replace it unless the fresh run is complete through 7305 d. |

`reference.json` is a cross-simulator reference, not a ResSim checkpoint: it is not accepted by
`CompositionalCase::restore`, and no scenario snapshot is taken from it. The five `stall_*.json`
states remain frozen seeds for their regressions.
